Color grading was built for specialists. Not anymore.

For decades, color correction belonged to colorists. Editors saw it as too technical, too intimidating, a world apart from the creative flow of storytelling. Adobe’s new Color Mode changes that, and FMC is partnering with Adobe to teach it.

Modern Post in Premiere is a two-day live online training, Sept. 29–30, built around the two biggest shifts in Premiere right now.

Register Now→
 
Day 1 Sept 29 · 9 AM–5:30 PM ET
Color Mode
Go from Color Mode foundations to matched shots, creative looks, and grades that stay organized across a full project. See how Color Mode fits with existing Lumetri work, then close the day with a live Q&A featuring Alexis Van Hurkman, Principal Product Manager of Color at Adobe.
View Day 1 Program
  
Day 2 Sept 30 · 9 AM–5:30 PM ET
AI-Assisted Post
Put AI to work across the post workflow. Find footage, compare takes, solve gaps in picture and sound, and plan with Firefly Boards, all while keeping the creative decisions in the editor’s hands.
