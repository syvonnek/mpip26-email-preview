One day to make color yours.

This is the same Color Mode that Adobe unveiled at NAB Show in April and that Alexis Van Hurkman keynoted at Post Production World Europe in May. On September 29, he takes your questions live.

Day 1 of Modern Post in Premiere moves from first principles to real production workflows:

 
Welcome  ·  9:00–10:30 AM ET
Color Mode Foundations & Color Management
Speaker: Luisa Winters
Establish the correct project, sequence, clip, monitoring, SDR/HDR, Log, wide-gamut, and tone-mapping pipeline.
 
Lab 1  ·  10:45 AM–12:15 PM ET
Primary Correction & Color Matching
Speaker: Eran Stern
Correct exposure, contrast, white balance, and saturation, then match cameras, scenes, interview angles, and skin tones.
 
Lab 2  ·  1:00–2:30 PM ET
Secondaries & Creative Grading
Speaker: Luisa Winters
Use Hue Isolation, hue/saturation/luminance controls, masks, tracking, Styles, and LUTs for targeted corrections and creative looks.
 
Lab 3  ·  2:30–4:00 PM ET
Grade Management at Scale
Speaker: Eran Stern
Manage corrections through Custom Groups and clip-, group-, and sequence-level grades.
 
Workflow  ·  4:15–4:45 PM ET
Transition & Compatibility: Color Mode & Lumetri
Speaker: Luisa Winters
Preserve and adjust legacy Lumetri grades while avoiding confusing hybrid workflows.
 
Live Q&A  ·  4:45–5:30 PM ET
Open Q&A with Alexis Van Hurkman
Moderator: Eran Stern
Bring real-world Color Mode and grading workflow questions to Alexis.
Why this day matters

You leave Day 1 with a complete Color Mode pipeline: the right project and monitoring setup, primary and secondary correction, targeted looks built with Styles and LUTs, and a system for managing grades at scale — plus how to carry existing Lumetri grades forward without a confusing hybrid workflow.
