Day 2 · AI-Assisted Post-Production
 
Session 1  ·  9:00–10:30 AM ET
Find the Story in the Footage
Search Premiere’s Media Intelligence and the Premiere AI Assistant by visual content, dialogue, sound, metadata, and similarity, then organize what you find into an edit-ready project.
 
Session 2  ·  10:45 AM–12:15 PM ET
AI as Your Assistant Editor
Identify and compare alternate takes, use Text-Based Editing to build selects and stringouts, and start assemblies faster. AI organizes the choices — you choose the performance.
 
Session 3  ·  1:00–2:30 PM ET
When the Edit Is Missing Something
Fix gaps with Generative Extend and Generative Media, find missing B-roll and inserts, match references, and use Object Masking and tracking — plus Content Credentials and when not to generate.
 
Session 4  ·  2:30–4:00 PM ET
From Rough Audio to Finished Sound
Clean up dialogue with Enhance Speech, generate sound effects and soundtracks, and mix faster with Remix and Auto Ducking.
 
Session 5  ·  4:15–4:45 PM ET
Visualize the Story with Firefly Boards
Build moodboards and storyboard-style frames for composition, locations, lighting, and wardrobe — and get the whole team aligned on the visual direction before you shoot.
 
Session 6  ·  4:45–5:30 PM ET
Q&A with Meagan Keane: Adobe’s Approach to AI in Premiere
Speaker: Meagan Keane
Director of Product Marketing, Adobe Professional Film & Video
Go behind the scenes on the philosophy driving Adobe’s AI investments in Premiere — built to clear the repetitive work and keep editors, not algorithms, in creative control.
