Build faster broadcast graphics
in Maxon Autograph.

More versions. More formats. More last-minute changes. The challenge is not creating the first graphic. It is keeping the project flexible when the brief changes.

On Wednesday, September 16, 2026 at 12 PM ET, join FMC and Maxon for a free live webinar, Get Started with Maxon Autograph: Build Broadcast Motion Graphics Faster.

Jeff Greenberg and Maxon Master Trainer Max Raharjo will build a broadcast-style project in Autograph and show how to move from design and animation to variations and revisions without starting over every time something changes.

Register Free
 

Meet your instructors
Jeff Greenberg

Max Raharjo
Maxon Master Trainer

Max is a Maxon Master Trainer with deep Autograph expertise and years of experience teaching creative professionals across the Maxon community.

 

What you will learn

See how Autograph handles the parts of broadcast graphics work that can slow a team down: adapting a design, creating multiple versions, responding to editorial changes, and delivering different formats from one flexible project.

 

Keep learning after the webinar

Every registrant receives the recording and the downloadable Autograph project file used in the session. Open the project after the webinar, explore how it was built, and apply the techniques to your own work.

Autograph is free for individual artists, including commercial projects.
