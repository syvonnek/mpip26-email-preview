Build broadcast graphics faster
with Maxon Autograph.

Broadcast and post teams are being asked to create more versions, support more formats, and respond to changes faster. On Monday, September 28, 2026 at 4:30 PM IST / 7 AM ET, FMC and Maxon are hosting a free live webinar focused on making that workflow easier.

Nick Harauz will be joined by François Grassard, the creator of Autograph, for Create Broadcast Graphics Faster with Maxon Autograph.

Together, they will build a broadcast-style project from start to finish and show how Autograph can help you animate, revise, and adapt graphics without rebuilding the project for every new requirement.
